# ⏱️ Work Time Calculator

A simple daily tracker that helps you calculate your half-day and full-day completion times based on your start time, and shows a live countdown until each milestone is reached.

![Daily Tracker](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-Daily%20Tracker-1e3a8a) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-active-brightgreen)

## ✨ Features

- **Start Time Input** — Set the time you began your workday.
- **Configurable Workday Durations**
  - **Half Day Duration** — Set custom hours & minutes (default: 4h 30m).
  - **Full Day Duration** — Set custom hours & minutes (default: 23h 59m).
- **Live Current Time Display** — Always-visible clock in the header.
- **Instant Calculation** — One click on **Calculate** computes all completion times.
- **Half Day / Full Day Completion Times** — Shows the exact clock time each milestone will be (or was) reached.
- **Live Working Time Counter** — Tracks total elapsed time since your start time, updating in real time.
- **Progress Bars** — Visual completion percentage for both half day and full day targets.
- **Remaining Time Countdown** — Live countdown showing time left until half day and full day completion.
- **Live Countdown Notifications** — On-screen confirmation of when each milestone completes.

## 🖥️ How It Works

1. Enter your **Start time** (e.g. `04:09`).
2. (Optional) Adjust the **Half day duration** and **Full day duration** under *Optional Workday Setup*.
3. Click **Calculate**.
4. View your results on the right panel:
   - Half day / Full day completion times
   - Live working time elapsed
   - Progress bars for both milestones
   - Remaining time countdowns (turns red once a milestone is reached)
